FAQ

Common Questions

Is there a joining fee or contract?

+
There is a one-time AED 150 registration fee for all new members. All plans are month-to-month with no lock-in contract. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. Annual plans are paid upfront and are non-refundable after the 14-day grace period.

Can I freeze or pause my membership?

+
Yes. All members can freeze their membership for up to 60 days per year (consecutive or split across the year). Freeze requests must be submitted 7 days in advance. Freezing is free for medical reasons with documentation and costs AED 50/month for other reasons.

Can I upgrade my plan at any time?

+
Absolutely. You can upgrade your plan at any point during your membership cycle and the difference will be prorated. Downgrading takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Speak to our front desk team or contact us via WhatsApp to make changes.

What are the opening hours?

+
Monday to Friday: 5:00AM – 11:00PM. Saturday and Sunday: 6:00AM – 10:00PM. Public holidays: 7:00AM – 9:00PM. We maintain reduced hours on certain UAE national holidays — check our social media or contact us for updates.

Do you offer couples or family memberships?

+
Yes. We offer a 10% discount on a second membership when two people join together at any tier. For family plans (3+ members), please contact us directly as we create custom packages to suit each family's needs and budget.

Is personal training included in all plans?

+
The Starter plan includes a complimentary one-time consultation session but does not include ongoing PT sessions. The Professional plan includes 4 sessions per month. The Elite plan includes unlimited personal training sessions with a dedicated head coach. Additional PT sessions can be purchased on the Starter plan at AED 180 per session.
