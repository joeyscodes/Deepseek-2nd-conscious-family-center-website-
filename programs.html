Functional Fitness

Intermediate 3–4 Days/Week 50–70 Min

True fitness goes beyond the gym floor. This program focuses on movement quality, mobility, core stability, and multi-planar conditioning — building a body that performs as well as it looks. Ideal for professionals, parents, and athletes who want longevity alongside performance.

  • Functional movement screening (FMS)
  • Mobility and corrective exercise programming

  • Core stability and anti-rotation work
  • Sport-specific conditioning options
